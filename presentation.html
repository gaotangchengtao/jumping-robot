02

Mechanism options

Elastic four-bar

Replaceable bands
Several joints to guide

Scissor + spring

Compact when folded
Many pinch points

Torsion-spring leg

Fewer moving links
High load at the pivot

A cam and catch can load and hold any of these mechanisms.

03

Loading force and travel

A longer lever trades
force for travel

Input work = ∫ Fhand ds

Friction and elastic losses reduce the energy available at take-off.

Measure force through the full loading stroke.

A low trigger force does not prove that loading stays below 20 N.

04

Proposed architecture

Elastic four-bar

Band tension drives the guided leg motion.

Coupled left and right legs

A common shaft constrains their motion.

Catch inside the body

The catch holds the loaded mechanism.

05

Loading and release

1 Load

Fold the handle to
tension the bands.

2 Hold

The catch engages.
The ready flag appears.

3 Release

Press the trigger.
The legs extend.

The catch carries the stored load; the trigger releases it.

06

Independent settings

Aim with the base

Rotate within a total 30° sector.

Set the preload

Use distinct mechanical stops.

Keep heading and stored energy as separate adjustments.

07

Design features

01

A handle that also covers the mechanism

The loading handle folds over the moving parts.

02

A ready flag linked to the catch

The indication follows engagement, not handle position.

03

Repeatable settings for a target game

Children can compare settings and predict landing points.

08

Protection and durability

Retain the elastic elements

Keep bands and attachments inside the enclosure.

Guard the moving joints

Keep fingers away during loading and release.

Replace the landing contacts

Use sacrificial pads at likely impact points.

09

Prototype development

V1

Mechanism rig

Check linkage travel, band attachment and take-off motion.

V2

Loading and catch

Measure peak hand force and verify full engagement.

V3

Enclosure and landing

Check access, impact paths and repeated use.

Change one major variable at a time and record the result.
